Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a mobile service or a physical salon?
Yes — we are a 100% mobile grooming service. Our fully-equipped, climate-controlled vans bring a professional grooming studio directly to your driveway. No car rides, no waiting rooms, no unfamiliar environments. Every appointment happens right outside your home.
Do I need to book an appointment in advance?
Yes! To guarantee our signature stress-free experience, we work by appointment only. This ensures every furry client receives our complete, undivided attention from start to finish.
How long does a full grooming session take?
Typically, a session lasts between 1.5 to 3 hours, depending on your pet's size, coat condition, and the selected style. We always respect their pace and will text you as soon as they are ready to go home!

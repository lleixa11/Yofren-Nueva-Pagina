Why this academy

Interested in grooming? This is the course for you.

Being a professional groomer is much more than bathing a dog or trimming its coat. A true professional knows the tools, understands what each pet needs, works with patience and safety, recognizes risky situations and gives clients an experience they want to repeat.

Complete technical training, from the basics to professional finishes.
Practice with real dogs, always under supervision.
Instructors who groom for a living, every single day.
Ready to get hired — or to start your own grooming business.
Our mission: to train responsible, well-prepared groomers who are fully committed to animal welfare.
What you'll master

More than cutting hair. A complete professional.

Great groomers win clients with their hands and with how they treat people. That's why our program covers four areas.

01

Becoming a Professional Groomer

The full technique, step by step, from the first brush to the final finish.

  • Bathing, drying and coat prep
  • Clipper cuts and scissoring
  • Nails, ears and hygiene
  • Safe handling of every temperament
02

Talking With Clients

Know what to ask, what to explain and how to set the right expectations.

  • The first conversation with a pet owner
  • Recommending the right service
  • Explaining results with confidence
  • Handling difficult situations calmly
03

Marketing & Sales

Get your work seen and keep your schedule full.

  • Building your professional image
  • Social media and before & after photos
  • Referrals and repeat bookings
  • The basics of running your own business
04

Customer Service & Ethics

The difference between a one-time client and a loyal one for years.

  • A five-star experience, start to finish
  • Punctuality, follow-up and trust
  • Professional ethics and limits
  • Knowing when to refer to a vet

Questions

Good to know

Do I need grooming experience? +

No. The program starts with the fundamentals and builds step by step. If you already groom, it will help you refine your technique and your client skills.

Will I work with real dogs? +

Yes. Every module includes hands-on practice with real dogs, always supervised by an instructor.

Does the program cover the business side? +

Yes. Beyond technique, you'll learn how to talk with clients, deliver great customer service, and market your work so you can get hired or start your own business.

Where are classes held? +

The academy is based in Miami, Florida.

How do I find out about dates and enrollment? +

Message us on WhatsApp or give us a call. We'll share the next start dates, schedule and everything you need to enroll.

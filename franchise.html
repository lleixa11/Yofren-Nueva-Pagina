Advantages

Why a Yofren Franchise Stands Out

We've spent 15+ years perfecting our model so you can start strong and scale fast — with every advantage built in from day one.

Low Overhead Costs

No brick-and-mortar lease, no expensive storefront. Your fully-equipped van is your business — keeping startup and operating costs significantly lower than traditional grooming salons.

Recurring Revenue

Pets need grooming every 4–6 weeks. This creates a predictable, subscription-like revenue stream that grows as your client base expands month after month.

Proven Brand Trust

Leverage 15+ years of brand reputation, 10K+ happy pets, and a 98% satisfaction rate. You don't start from zero — you start with credibility.

Scalable Growth

Start with one van, then add routes, hire more groomers, and expand your territory. Our system is built to scale with your ambition.

Flexible Schedule

Be your own boss, set your own hours, and build your week around your life — not the other way around. Work-life balance is built into the model.

Recession-Resistant

What's Fueling the Growth?

67%

Record Pet Ownership

67% of U.S. households now own a pet — the highest ever recorded. More pets means more demand for professional grooming.

"Pet Humanization" Trend

Owners increasingly treat pets as family members, spending more on premium grooming, health checks, and spa-like experiences.

Mobile Grooming Boom

Mobile grooming is the fastest-growing segment — driven by convenience, less pet stress, and the post-pandemic preference for at-home services.

Recession-Proof Spending

Even in economic downturns, pet care spending stays strong. Grooming is a necessity, not a luxury — ensuring consistent demand year-round.

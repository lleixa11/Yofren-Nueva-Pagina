Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Yofren Master Pet Grooming uses them, and your choices regarding cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly and remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies, primarily:

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function (e.g., form submission, security)
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings
  • Third-party cookies from Google (Fonts) and Formspree (form processing)

3. Third-Party Cookies

  • Google Fonts: loads fonts from Google servers, which may log your IP address.
  • Formspree: processes our contact and franchise forms. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Moego: our booking platform (opens in a new tab). See Moego.

4. Analytics

We do not currently use tracking analytics cookies. If we add them in the future, this policy will be updated.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Note: disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

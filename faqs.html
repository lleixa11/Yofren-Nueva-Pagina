General

Yofren Master Pet Grooming is a premium mobile grooming franchise based in South Florida. Instead of a physical salon, each franchisee operates a fully-equipped grooming van and provides one-on-one, stress-free grooming directly at the client's home. We've been in the industry since 2010 and are now expanding our proven model across the U.S.

No prior grooming experience is required. We provide comprehensive hands-on training as part of your onboarding. What we do look for is a genuine love for animals, a strong work ethic, and a willingness to follow our proven system. The technical skills are teachable — the right character is what matters most.

This is designed as a full-time business ownership opportunity. Running a Yofren Master franchise means managing routes, building a client base, and growing your territory — which requires consistent, dedicated effort. That said, the flexibility of the mobile model does give you more control over your schedule than a traditional brick-and-mortar business.

Three things set us apart: the mobile-first model eliminates rent and fixed location costs; our community-first culture means you're joining a network of people who genuinely care about animal wellness — not just a transactional business relationship; and our lower barrier to entry makes ownership accessible without sacrificing a premium service experience.

Investment

The estimated total investment ranges from $50,000 to $80,000, which includes the franchise fee, fully equipped grooming van, initial marketing, and training. You'll need a minimum of $30,000 in liquid capital and a net worth of at least $100,000. Exact figures are detailed in the Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) provided after your initial consultation.

Financing options may be available through third-party lenders. We can point you to partners who have experience working with franchise applicants. SBA loans are also a commonly used path for franchise investment. We'll discuss financing options during the qualification process so you can find the best fit for your situation.

Ongoing fees include a royalty fee calculated as a percentage of your gross revenue. There is no monthly rent or lease — one of the major financial advantages of the mobile model. All ongoing fee structures are fully disclosed in the FDD before you sign anything.

Your initial investment covers the franchise fee, a fully equipped grooming van, comprehensive training and onboarding, and a marketing launch package to help you build your client base from day one. You won't need to source equipment or build out a location — everything you need to operate is included.

Training & Support

Our onboarding program covers everything you need before your first day in the field: grooming techniques and pet handling, van operation and maintenance, route planning and scheduling, client communication and retention strategies, and basic business management using our tools. Training is hands-on and completed before you launch — you won't be figuring things out on the job.

You're not on your own once you launch. We provide continued operational support, marketing resources, access to our franchisee community, and regular check-ins from our team. As your business grows and you consider adding vans or hiring staff, we'll be there to help you navigate each stage of growth.

Training duration varies based on your background and how quickly you absorb the material, but most franchisees complete the initial program within a few weeks. The goal is to make sure you feel genuinely confident before your launch day — not just technically prepared, but comfortable with clients and ready to represent the brand.

Operations

A standard valid driver's license with a clean driving record is required. Beyond that, specific licensing requirements vary by state and municipality — we'll guide you through exactly what's needed in your area during the onboarding process so you're fully compliant before you hit the road.

Your marketing launch package includes tools and strategies to help you acquire your first clients. Long-term, the mobile model is a natural retention engine — clients love the convenience and their pets love the one-on-one environment. Word-of-mouth and repeat bookings are the primary drivers of a healthy Yofren business. We also provide digital marketing resources and local promotion guidance.

Most franchisees start owner-operated and scale from there. As your territory grows, you can add vans and bring on licensed groomers or drivers — that's actually the scalability model we encourage. We'll support you through hiring and managing staff when you're ready to expand.

Routine maintenance is part of running a van-based business, and we train you on basic upkeep. For major repairs, we provide guidance on preferred service partners and contingency protocols so client appointments are minimally disrupted. As your business grows, having more than one van also protects against downtime.

Territory

Yes. Each franchisee is assigned a defined geographic territory. No other Yofren franchise will be granted within your territory, giving you the market protection you need to build a strong, loyal client base. Territory boundaries are defined during the application process based on population density and demand.

We are actively expanding across the United States, with current focus areas in Florida and surrounding states. Territory availability changes as franchises are granted, so the best way to find out what's open near you is to submit an inquiry — we'll share a current availability map during your consultation.

Absolutely. Multi-territory ownership is something we actively support for franchisees who have successfully established their first territory. Adding more vans and expanding into adjacent areas is exactly the growth path we help our top performers pursue. We'll work with you on a scaling plan when you're ready.

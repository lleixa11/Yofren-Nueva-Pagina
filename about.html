Our Story

How it all began

It started with one simple idea: pets deserve the same comfort, patience, and dignity we'd want for ourselves. What began as one groomer with a genuine passion for animals has grown into a trusted, doorstep grooming service families across South Florida rely on.

For over 15 years, Yofren Master Pet Grooming has been built on a simple promise: no cages, no stress, no rushed appointments — just calm, one-on-one care, delivered right to your driveway.

Today, our vans serve families across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Naples, but the mission hasn't changed since day one: treat every pet like family.

Hello, I'm

Yofren

Founder & Master Groomer

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in professional pet grooming, Yofren turned his deep passion for animals into a brand built on trust, quality, and genuine care.

He founded Yofren Master Pet Grooming with a clear mission: to bring professional, stress-free grooming directly to every pet's doorstep. From day one, his approach has centered on treating every pet as family — with patience, respect, and the highest standards of hygiene and safety.

Yofren personally designed the service protocols, trained the grooming team, and built the mobile routes that allow the brand to cover South Florida efficiently. His commitment to humane, cage-free grooming and one-on-one attention has earned the loyalty of thousands of families across the region.
